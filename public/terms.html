Practical usage terms and current limitations for this project.

Last updated: October 2026

Project scope

ClassHub is an offline-first classroom resource sharing project. The classroom application is intended for a teacher-managed local network. The public website provides product information and a link to the classroom interface.

Network and capacity

ClassHub does not provide Wi-Fi service, guarantee connectivity, or guarantee a number of supported devices. Classroom capacity depends on the local network hardware. The scheduler manages application-level resource access only.

Uploads and classroom content

Teachers are responsible for content they publish and files they upload, including ensuring they have the right to share them. Uploaded material is stored on the configured server filesystem; the operator must protect and back up persistent storage.

Availability and data

The project is provided as-is for evaluation and deployment. Operators are responsible for secure configuration, backups, access to the host, and compliance with applicable requirements. Hosting platforms with ephemeral disks may remove local database and upload files when an instance restarts or is replaced.

External links

Links to portfolio and social sites lead to services operated by others. Their own policies and terms apply when those links are used.
